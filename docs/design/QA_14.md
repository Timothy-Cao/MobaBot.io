# MobaBot.io 0.14 — Expedition handoff

8 September 2026. This is the shipped implementation record; `SYSTEMS_REFINEMENT_12.md` is historical. Research, source quality and design reasoning are in [EXPEDITION_RESEARCH_14.md](EXPEDITION_RESEARCH_14.md). Current launch instructions and controls are in the root README.

## Outcome and scope

The normal project entry point is now `src/salvage/expedition.tscn`. The eight-stage direction is playable rather than a roadmap: three classes, 22 rounds, three shops, A0–A5, chest-based discoveries, rank/rarity progression, 48 mastery nodes, eight equipment slots and five sets containing 40 items. Existing `workshop.tscn` and the original Neon Collector scene remain intact for regression/history.

There are 34 active choices in the normal discovery catalog: 28 additions plus Impact bolt, Welding torch, Reactor drop, Core cutter, Ghost drive and Blink. The old homing-salvo/shield/turret-centered preset is removed from the normal active gallery and chest pool, but its code remains for compatibility tests. There are 15 passive choices, including seven new mechanisms. This is an original mechanical adaptation of the user's notes, not a recreation of named League champions.

The permanent progression decision is equipment, banked credits and ascension access. Mastery stays run-only to preserve build experimentation and avoid stacking two unlimited permanent power systems. The equipment collection is capped and lower ascensions remain farmable. This is a deliberate interpretation of the user's permission to choose, not an unresolved blocker.

The seven new powered passives are Split barrel (two secondary basic bolts), Plate magazine (plant up to 12 plates), Third contact (per-target three-hit proc with expiry), Flywheel (movement buildup and collision payoff), Hop drive (next commanded move hops after a basic), Life converter (energy/hull exchange, reverse floor at 30% hull) and Shoulder drones (two small mounted shooters). Mounted drones are outside major summon capacity. The existing single pet slot remains separate.

Shunpo was explicitly discarded and is not implemented. Poison trail has energy upkeep only while enabled and no self-damage. “Snowball” became Ballast roll, “bastion heal” became interruptible Self repair, and “drop SFX potency” was treated as presentation—not a stat requiring purchased skill points. These interpretations were announced during the work. Mounted shots are small simultaneous pairs, not separately controllable pets. Mirror effects approximate the source's base damage through shared line/blade primitives; they do not recreate every source projectile or inherit recursive procs.

Ranks improve numerical power/recharge. Rank 5 and 10 effects are wired to actual ranges, widths, radii, healing, barrier duration/width or summon lifetime. Tooltips report the relevant metric. Rarity is distinct from rank. Selected complex discoveries have lower weights; chest cards use different slots, preserve unique ability IDs and cannot break Orbit/Ricochet prerequisites. First locked-slot discoveries favor the selected class/loadout. Later rewards can replace skills; Q always starts as Impact bolt.

## Campaign and progression

The route matches the requested example: 3/3/3/2/2/4/3/2 rounds across stages 1–8. Shops follow stages 2, 5 and 7. Neutral rounds last 50 seconds, loot rounds 40, and boss rounds begin with a 35-second lead-in. Modal decisions pause the simulation. Clearing gathers remaining drops, restores 20% maximum hull and refills energy.

Gunner gets ranged basics and short movement boosts after commanded attacks; Brawler gets stronger close basics, speed, hull, resistance and regeneration; Engineer starts with two major constructs and summon power/lifetime bonuses. All start with gun/Q plus D/F safety tools. W/E/R/T and remaining passive slots now come from chests, not a timed unlock script.

Power levels grant rank choices and two mastery points after the initial point. Every fifth level grants a discovery chest. Bosses/wardens and some elites drop collectible chests; round completion grants more, with loot rounds richer. Duplicate actives give two ranks; rank-cap overflow pays field currency. Passive duplicates give field currency. Equipment is a rarer additional outcome, not guaranteed on every ordinary kill.

Mastery has 48 nodes in six branches, prerequisite lanes and per-node ranks. It contains damage, basic damage/rate/range, mobility, tenacity, resistance, hull, regeneration, energy, pickup radius, XP, drop luck, summon power/lifetime/capacity, stun and aftershock effects. Only current-run points are spendable. Reset points is available at camp without a fee or free-healing exploit.

The new hull model uses fractional values and a base around 100 rather than five integer hits. Resistance feeds one central damage formula; gear and mastery feed a shared stat budget. Extreme speed, recharge, damage, drop and summon values are capped. Flywheel and Ballast roll have damage mitigation while active. D remains a clear escape rather than being mandatory to maintain passive DPS.

Eight boss configurations reuse the existing large industrial body with differing charge, fan, shell and ring sequences, reinforcements and ascension pressure. The final boss is tougher and combines those patterns. New world barriers support local end-point avoidance, projectile blocking and explicit vault/phasing exceptions; large bosses can break nearby barriers.

## Persistent equipment and save protection

Slots: helmet, chest, legs, boots, charm, ring, flower and cape. Sets: Courier, Bastion, Dynamo, Relay and Reclaimer. Each has two/four-piece effects; higher-tier accessories also have an individual specialty. Only equipped items apply. Set proc cooldowns are independent.

Reroll costs 35 banked credits. Star N costs N spare copies plus 25 × N credits, up to five stars; one copy stays owned. Each star adds 20% of base stats. Field credits buy permanent equipment during shops. Clear rewards bank without granting the same receipt again. Camp equipment changes update the current build; home changes apply to new runs.

`mobabot_expedition.json` stores the new profile and a cleared-round checkpoint. The old `mobabot_equipment.json` is read-only import input, preserved with its exact original snapshot/mapping recorded in the new data. Existing settings/music are preserved. No automated test awards permanent loot.

Checkpoint data includes route, class, ascension, skills/ranks/rarities, mastery, XP, resources, consumables, toggle modes, pending discoveries, field money, shop stock and random-generator states encoded as strings to preserve 64-bit precision. Resume restores a camp; it does not reconstruct the exact fight you closed. Resource upgrades and replacement-skill charge capacities survive restoration. Sold shop slots remain sold.

New runs ask before replacing an existing checkpoint. Defeat clears the checkpoint but keeps banked equipment. Invalid saves are not overwritten. Write validation, temporary replacement and rollback protect transactions; failures remain visible. The tests include an isolated JSON save/load round trip under ignored `output/`, then remove that test file. Player saves are never used as test output.

## Presentation and subtraction

The foundry title artwork stays. New skills and gear extend the native icon family rather than introduce a new art style. Forty items are eight silhouettes with five material/motif variants, not forty paintings. No paid asset dependency or new AI-image service was required.

Home retains five actions. Class/ascension setup is one screen. Loadout remains Abilities/Passives with bounded nine-item galleries; unavailable start-Q edits are visibly disabled. Build remains Overview/Mastery. Equipment shows fitted slots, one filtered collection and one signed comparison including stats that would be lost. Mechanics and set details stay on hover/focus. Settings remains four options plus Controls.

HUD keeps resources together and distinguishes QWER from DF/T. Locked slots no longer advertise obsolete countdowns. The old three-level rail is hidden for expeditions; actual stage and boss names replace legacy labels. Recast windows show remaining time. Combat effects preserve hostile readability, and new targeting previews distinguish cones, center-critical areas, wall spans and third-thrust reach.

Visual QA caught and fixed a self-intersecting returning-blade polygon, a long Orbital entry upgrade value overflowing its card, misleading inner circles on ordinary artillery impacts, and overly repetitive mastery icons. Upgrade number formatting removes unnecessary decimal zeros. New equipment comparison rows include negative deltas. No full-screen flashes or world shake were added.

## Evaluation, remaining work and playtest priorities

The system expansion is implemented and mechanically testable. It now offers genuine alternatives to passive-only play: rim spacing, returning paths, targeted resets, cast commitments, summon placement and resource modes. The important remaining question is whether the combination feels good—not whether more systems can be added.

Known limits, not hidden completed work:

- Three reused arena sectors and one major boss art family; not eight individually authored environments or eight unique boss illustrations.
- Local segment avoidance, not full navmesh routing. Complex overlapping barriers, crowd compression and high-speed wall approaches need human scrutiny.
- Static native equipment variants, not custom character-worn equipment art or bespoke animation sheets.
- Checkpoints at completed rounds, not exact mid-combat saves; no cloud sync.
- Balance is provisional. A0 with starter gear must be viable for a human without forcing a grind. One deterministic bot seed cannot establish fairness, class parity or retention.
- The eight-stage route adds many paused choices. If it drags, reduce repeated upgrade/chest interruptions before adding further progression tracks.
- Supply sound intensity remains presentation, not a purchasable gameplay stat. No Shunpo. Named-champion inspiration was adapted, not copied ability-for-ability.
- Release packaging, broader hardware/window testing, audio listening and confirmation of music rights remain before public distribution.

No user input blocks testing. Start with Gunner on A0, then compare Brawler. Most useful feedback: when the first three rounds become repetitive, whether aimed casts justify the attention, which movement action feels unreliable, and whether a chest replacement feels like an exciting option or a loss of your build. Keep numerical tuning in `skill_catalog.gd`, `expedition.gd`, `expedition_tree.gd` and `expedition_gear.gd`; resist adding another menu to solve a pacing issue.
