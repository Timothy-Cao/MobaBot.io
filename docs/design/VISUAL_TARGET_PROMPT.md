# Workshop salvager visual target

Purpose: one preview-only art-direction board, not production sprites or gameplay evidence.

Execution: built-in image generation. First attempt failed with a network error. One bounded retry remained pending and its wait was stopped; no completed image was available for inspection or delivery. No CLI/API fallback, external account or paid service setup used.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: art-direction feasibility board for an original Windows 2D top-down survivor roguelite. Primary request: depict a charming tiny salvage hover-machine in a toy-like repair workshop, showing how simple reusable art can support an escalating power fantasy. This is a practical restrained game-art target, not a lavish cinematic painting. Create one landscape concept board with a narrow top strip of isolated reusable asset designs and two equally sized top-down gameplay-like panels below, labeled only "START" and "POWERED UP"; a small understated heading reads "WORKSHOP SALVAGER — VISUAL TARGET". Top strip: one compact cream-and-teal rounded salvage robot body with black inset face and two simple eye lights, no arms or legs, a small horseshoe magnet accessory; three distinct hostile machine silhouettes (round red-orange bumper drone, pointed coral rammer, square plum spitter with a large visible nozzle); separate saw disc, spring coil, gold bolt pickup. All use the same crisp dark outline, simple flat two-tone shading, broad color shapes, very restrained texture, orthographic elevated top-down 2D illustrated cutout view, no perspective grid, no photorealism, no pixel art. Robot face remains front-facing and readable. Assets must look practical at 32 to 48 pixels on screen. Left panel START: small robot in a muted gray-blue workshop floor arena with sparse large floor seams, a few discarded screws at distant edges, three incoming machine enemies, simple automatic bolt shot and two nearby gold scrap pickups. Right panel POWERED UP: EXACT SAME SIZE and identity player body and camera scale, now surrounded by a clearly readable ring of six chunky reclaimed saw/metal modules and a second outward ricochet attack, two groups of about eight enemies popping into a FEW bold fragments with clean impact shapes. Show open navigable floor and the player clearly, with only two coral danger telegraphs distinguishable from gold player attacks. Most of the background stays low contrast and sparse. The visual reward is a little machine with a ridiculous but comprehensible orbiting arsenal, not a giant humanoid transformation. No complex walking or acting poses, no elaborate machinery detail, no item cards, no logos, no busy HUD, no tiny text, no cinematic bloom, no vast particle fog, no additional side characters. Overall tone: tactile, mischievous, energetic, polished but achievable using sprites, geometry and motion. Clearly maintain consistent line weight and palette across the asset strip and both panels.

## Acceptance notes

- Inspect hero identity and body scale across both views, broad silhouettes, consistent camera/palette and readable threats.
- Do not adopt extra generated details as requirements. Keep the written design authoritative if the image drifts.
- A successful single board validates only a possible static visual direction, not animation, small-scale gameplay readability or asset-production repeatability.
