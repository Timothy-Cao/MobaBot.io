# MobaBot.io 0.11 — Foundry presentation pass

## Outcome

The title-screen collage is replaced by a cohesive foundry launch bay. Navigation, equipment, mastery, rewards and combat HUD share the same graphite, teal, cream and brass language. This is a presentation pass over the 0.10 combat systems, not a new campaign or balance reset. Relaunch the game to load **0.11 Foundry Demo**.

Research and bounded reference observations: [presentation research](PRESENTATION_RESEARCH_11.md). Exact generated-art prompt and provenance: [menu asset record](../../assets/menu/PROVENANCE.md). Art direction: [schema 1.6](ART_STYLE_SCHEMA.md).

## Changes

- **Title:** original full-screen foundry illustration, readable native title lockup, short left navigation, prominent Play and small three-encounter route. Removed the floating-equipment collage and permanent control instruction. The art is static, aspect-preserving and mipmapped; no menu camera shake or parallax.
- **Equipment:** bot assembly schematic highlights Core/Chassis/Drive; fitted slots sit beside a six-item collection grouped by slot. One inspector separates base stats, rolled bonus, stars and signed changes versus the fitted item. Costs and rules live on hover; failures remain visible. Unowned, insufficient-copy and max-star states remain blocked. No new economy or slot types.
- **Mastery:** nine existing nodes organized into three connected routes; rank badges, available markers and larger endpoints. One stable detail pane responds to mouse or keyboard focus and shows current-to-next contribution. Purchasing keeps focus. Main-menu access opens the read-only tree directly; in-run points still require Tab and never create another popup.
- **Upgrades:** three icon-led cards, current-kit strip, rank pips and two relevant numerical gains. Rank-5/10 choices have a distinct milestone treatment and surface effect-size changes. Complete numerical detail remains on hover. Repair preview is capped to actual maximum health.
- **Encounter rewards:** cleaner rarity cards and short recovery summary. Damage previews now include equipment and mastery, exactly matching the promoted ability. Utility abilities show recharge instead of misleading damage increases.
- **Shared UI:** stronger heading face, darker planes, square major panels, restrained corner accents and underline tabs in Build/Loadout. Removed noisy diagonal stripes behind ability glyphs. Existing icons and rank inspection mechanics are retained, not replaced with unrelated artwork.
- **Combat HUD:** less vertical obstruction; QWER remain primary, passives/DF/T distinct. Health/energy/XP remain together. Small three-sector rail replaces repeated mission prose. Repair/energy consumables now have clickable icons as well as keys 5/6. Their handlers reject use outside running combat.
- **Correctness:** raised modal layer covers HUD counters; energy-cell use no longer announces a repair; focused lightning status fits its tile. Result screen no longer announces successful local saving as routine clutter; unsaved state remains visible.
- **Audio:** short, quiet native hover/confirmation cues respect mute. Skip MP3 startup on the Dummy driver to avoid retaining unavailable playback. User music is unchanged.
- **Maintainability:** separated menu, equipment, mastery and upgrade presentation into small view files. Added pure equipment-comparison and ability-preview helpers, repeatable edge-state fixtures and automated tests. No engine/cache, screenshots or player records belong in Git.

## Verification

Godot 4.7.2, Windows, Compatibility renderer / RTX 5070. Full `scripts/check.ps1`: smoke plus **1,740 checks**, zero failures. New 0.11 suite: **178 checks**. Final suite log contains no errors or warnings. Separate layout audit: **3,371 visible labels**, zero measured width issues. A width audit cannot detect every overlap; actual rendered screens were also inspected.

New tests exercise native directional focus, menu routes, read-only mastery, focus retention after purchase, all equipment stat/affix combinations against applied simulation values, rarity previews with gear/mastery, unowned equipment, memory-only star transactions, combat-only consumables, modal depth, focused lightning text and Dummy music cleanup. Earlier suites still cover movement/shaking, channel mechanics, damage geometry, charges, boss behavior, persistence validation and progression.

Visual review included 1600×900 home, equipment, mastery, loadout, stats, rank tree, gameplay, ordinary upgrades and encounter rewards; 1280×720 equipment comparisons, max stars and rank-5 cards; 1920×1080 title composition. Intermediate defects were fixed and recaptured: loose logo suffix spacing, consumable labels above modals, dense icon backgrounds, incorrect reward preview multipliers and a fixture that initially failed to populate the model's upgrade ranks. These are rendered fixtures, not a claim of human usability testing. Capture/log evidence remains in ignored `output/playtest-v11/`.

Both behavior probes were rerun with seeds 2407–2409 and normal starter equipment. Results match 0.10 exactly:

| Policy | Results |
| --- | --- |
| Idle | Lost at 8.77–10.12s |
| Stationary casting | Lost at 11.58–13.55s |
| Moving, indiscriminate casting | Lost at 124.47 / 291.77 / 141.08s |
| Moving, passive-only | Lost at 27.10 / 141.57 / 107.55s |
| Adaptive | Won at 291.40 / 294.83 / 298.43s; 10 / 17 / 8 damage taken |

A real-renderer autoplay run reached Level 2 and lost at 122.42s, 299 kills and 6 damage taken, with no runtime errors. It inherited the local saved W = Homing salvo / Drone loadout, so it is not directly comparable to the fixed-preset probes. Recorded frame intervals were 7.44ms median / 11.50ms p95, with 40 peak enemies on this RTX 5070; this is not a lower-end benchmark or a rendered three-level completion.

No automated run awards permanent equipment or credits. Equipment/settings mutations in fixtures use memory only. The rendered autoplay appended one diagnostic run record through the existing logger; it did not bank loot. Actual-profile storage mutation tests were not run.

## Limitations and next review

- Windows WASAPI could not open an audio output during initial rendering. Visual QA used Dummy audio; **no listening or mix-quality claim**. No system audio settings were changed.
- Human judgement remains necessary: title-art appeal, ease of finding mastery/gear information, readability during pressure, laser steering and fairness of the three-hull boss attacks. Bot wins do not establish fun or fair difficulty.
- The title illustration deliberately has richer staging than combat shapes. Mk I/II equipment shares functional silhouettes with explicit labels; six unique paintings are not implied.
- Text scaling/accessibility certification, a packaged Windows export, lower-end hardware testing and music-rights confirmation remain release work. No further level expansion is needed before playtesting this slice.
- An automatic permission review timed out during this pass; a retry succeeded. There is no remaining implementation question requiring the creator to unblock local testing.

## Suggested playtest

Relaunch via `Play MobaBot.io.cmd`. Look at Equipment and the main-menu Mastery preview, then play one run. The most useful feedback is whether the new presentation feels coherent, whether selected-item tradeoffs are obvious, and whether you can read cooldowns and passive states without looking away from threats for too long.
