# 0.17 · Field Test

9 September 2026. Implements the owner's queued pacing, combat and testing pass. This supersedes 0.16's classes, short rounds, replacement rules and hold-Tab behavior. Legacy scenes/tests deliberately retain old rules; the main expedition enables `rules17`.

## Changes

1. **Round rhythm:** 180 seconds of survival, then a miniboss on neutral/loot rounds or the existing boss on boss rounds. The HUD identifies stage.round, counts down survival, then shows ELITE/BOSS. Defeat starts **12 seconds of safe collection** with LOOT countdown. Hostiles retire, spawning stops, and XP/chest menus wait. Remaining drops are collected when camp opens.
2. **XP:** requirements increase smoothly: ×1.25 through level 5, ×1.50 at level 20, ×1.75 at level 35 and above. Smoothstep interpolation between anchors. This interprets “slower” as more XP required, not a multiplicative reduction in XP income. Existing checkpoints migrate only the remaining threshold once.
3. **Heavy commanded attacks:** 530 base range, 0.7 shots/sec, 20.09 damage per shot, 0.2-second cancellable windup. Move/S cancels before release; firing is a real commitment. Targets behind cover are approached until line of fire is clear. Bigger brass-core projectiles, mechanical charging/recoil, hit sparks and a lower-pitched cue distinguish them from Q and the automatic gun.
4. **Independent gun:** small shots, 265 range, 1.2 damage every 0.16 seconds (−20%). Sniper also receives the 20% reduction. Its clock remains independent; S cannot disable it. Quiet rapid cues sit below heavy basics and Q in the mix.
5. **Close-range payoff:** torch, Guard sweep, Rim cutter, Piston thrust, Repulsor and Tractor cone gain 60% direct damage and 15% actual geometry scale. Q/laser damage is unchanged. Casting opens the robot's barrel assembly; Guard sweep has a moving blade stroke. Existing rank milestones still modify real geometry. No full-screen flashes or added active-skill input delay.
6. **Passive clarity:** current MG/SNP, near/far orbit, fast/long coil and on/off states appear on the existing tiles. Mechanics remain in hover details.
7. **Terrain:** distributed, disconnected rail-cover islands across the arena. Wide passages and clear starting space; no enclosed rooms. Fixed distant-click routing to consider body clearance, the nearest blocking wall and progress beyond endpoints. Twenty long cross-map paths pass. This is a gentle mostly-open density pass, not a measured claim that exactly 20% of floor area is solid.
8. **Three ranged enemies:** Arc lancer charges a locked line for 0.8s, then fires a 40-hull base beam; Burst battery warns for 0.65s then sweeps five 20-hull projectiles; Bomb carrier places three offset, staggered 40-hull blasts. Existing resistance/ascension apply. They start attacks inside the player-centered view, not the detached camera. Normal waves add at most one every 26 seconds, with at most three alive. Laser respects cover; killing a bomber cancels its pending bombs. Threat tells retain a final rendering pass in normal/reduced effects.
9. **No classes:** one shared pool, one summon base capacity, no class-specific stat bonuses. Start with MG/Q/D/F. Old class fields remain solely for compatibility with historical saves and regression fixtures.
10. **Never discard learned skills:** empty-key placement during discovery; if full, learn into storage. At camp, swap bindings or explicitly fit a stored tool. The displaced skill remains stored with rank/rarity. Duplicate stored actives upgrade their stored rank; duplicate passives improve their shared track. Orbit/Ricochet dependency remains protected. No combat-time rearranging. Library contents validate and survive checkpoints.
11. **Practice:** separate home action; fit any discoverable skill on a legal key at rank 0/5/10; spawn 1/5/10/25/50/100 selected enemies, dummies or bosses. Independent god mode, infinite energy and instant recharge; Clear removes test threats/effects and restores resources. Tab toggles play/configure. Does not spend collection items, award permanent loot or replace the actual checkpoint.
12. **Tab toggles Build** in the main run. Release no longer dismisses it.
13. **Layered menu:** generated clean foundry background plus genuinely transparent robot cutout, with restrained native hover and two animated jets. Reduced effects freezes this motion. Original v1 artwork and Base icons are preserved. Exact prompts/hashes are in `assets/menu/LAYERS_PROVENANCE.md`.
14. **Ability review artifact:** `docs/review/abilities.html` contains all 49 current icons, descriptions, category/search filters, Keep/Rework/Drop, notes, JSON export and print styling. Regenerate with `scripts/build_ability_catalog.gd`. Static icons do not demonstrate combat animation; use Practice for that.

## Balance interpretation and measurements

The user's specific targets conflict with the approximate 75% gun/basic ratio. We prioritized MG −20% and basic DPS = 1.5× the old MG. At rank 0, before gear/accuracy:

| Weapon | Theoretical single-target DPS |
| --- | ---: |
| Previous MG | 9.375 |
| New MG | 7.5 |
| New commanded basic | 14.0625 |

Thus MG is **53.3%** of basic DPS when both hit, not 75%. Basic windup is part of the firing interval, not an extra 0.2 seconds added to it. These are ceiling rates, not observed combat damage.

The revised 18-skill probe matches every stationary per-cast ceiling. Against its 12 prescribed moving trajectories, torch lands 58.3% with fixed aim versus 100% with tracking; Q 33.3% versus 100% with fixture-perfect lead; laser 33.6% versus 90.1% with tracking. Cone/rim tools land fully at the fixture's intentionally close spacing. These are diagnostic geometric policies, **not estimates of average player accuracy** or comprehensive crowd-control value. Damage/recharge is not a fair standalone ranking for area damage, stored charges, healing or mobility.

Fixed seed 7127, starter equipment, actual A0 health: idle policy lost at 18.1s; casting/evasion lost at 128.1s; a simple heavy-basic commitment policy lost at 90.3s. All were in round 1. Do not interpret this as a representative human win rate. The extra commitment can be costly, and the new round length substantially delays the first safe camp. Preserve the owner's prior positive difficulty feedback; test rhythm and kiting before blanket damage tuning.

Final revised A5 artificial-health soak completed all 22 rounds: **4,440.5 simulated seconds (74 minutes), 21,164 kills, level 143, peak 78 enemies, p95 simulation step 1,276µs, max 6,158µs** on this machine. Rendering/GPU, human decisions and pause time are excluded. This demonstrates route completion under one policy, not balance or fun. Twenty-two survival phases alone total 66 minutes; first-stage survival alone totals nine minutes. This is the largest open pacing risk.

## Verification and limits

- Final `scripts/check.ps1`: all **21** scripts passed, zero logged engine errors. Legacy fixtures are preserved, not relabeled as new-rule coverage.
- New refinement fixture: **212 checks**, including 20 cross-map routes, XP cap, timed miniboss, uninterrupted collection, windup/cancel, MG independence, all three shooter cycles, library retention/malformed saves/resume, real mouse-event interaction, Tab toggling and practice checkpoint isolation.
- Revised skill visual fixture: **2,252 checks**, ranks 0/5/10 and normal/reduced effects; rendering must not change simulation and effect pools stay bounded. Rendered close-range captures and menu/practice/threat captures are reviewed separately from assertions.
- Keyboard UI: 355 labels, zero width issues. Offline HTML browser QA: 49 entries, search/category, persisted notes, review download and no narrow-screen horizontal overflow. Desktop artifact inspected visually.
- Both `--refined` legacy behavior probes ran: naive policies lose; adaptive legacy policies can finish. They are not the new 22-round campaign.
- Menu source alpha is explicitly tested. The test intentionally loads the raw PNG for source inspection; Godot's raw-image export warning is about that fixture, not runtime menu loading, which uses imported textures.

No external dependency or user answer blocks testing. Remaining limits: no clean-machine/export certification, no low-end GPU claim, no listening verdict, no human approval of new attack timing or every skill animation. Rail routing is a local detour algorithm, not a general navigation mesh for arbitrary mazes. The three shooters are new native silhouettes, not unique painted sprite sheets. Practice currently tests skills/ranks/enemies, not an equipment editor or combat recording tool.

## Research applied

Riot's [13.22 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-au/news/game-updates/patch-13-22-notes/) treat missile speed and attack animation snappiness as basic-attack feel concerns. Our exact range, cadence and windup come from the owner, not copied champion balance. Its [Practice Tool additions in 10.8](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-au/news/game-updates/patch-10-8-notes/) support separating repeatable encounter setup from normal progression; our spawn panel is a scoped implementation of that idea. [Clarity in League](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/clarity-in-league/) informs silhouette, effect priority and truthful geometry. These sources inform principles, not proof this prototype is polished.
