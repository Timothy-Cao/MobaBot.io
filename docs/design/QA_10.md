# MobaBot.io 0.10 — playtest handoff

Ready for another human playtest. Stage 1 remains three levels. No implementation blockers; difficulty and visual taste still need human judgement.

## What changed

- **Combat:** larger, faster Foreman with a four-attack sequence, predictive charge, shell placements, a gapped radial volley, reinforcement waves and a half-health overclock. Shorter readable tells and stronger wardens. Heavy contact and boss shots hit for 2 hull; boss charges hit for 3. Tanks and radial shots can briefly slow the player.
- **Q / E / R:** Q has a finned missile silhouette, broad exhaust and distinct impact. E is the old area nuke. R is a five-second, rooted laser with a continuous hum; right-click steers with acceleration and capped turn speed. R again cancels; D/F can interrupt into an escape. Width and effect size change at ranks 5/10; numerical ranks improve damage and cooldown.
- **D:** three seconds of damage/slow immunity plus movement speed. Higher milestones extend speed, not immunity.
- **Passives:** default Auto bolt, Scrap orbit, Arc coil, Reactive plating. Arc coil cycles chain / focused strike / off. Collection pulse, Ricochet and Recoil shell remain alternatives. Default upkeep is 10 energy/sec against 8/sec base regen; equipment, reactor ranks and mastery can improve it. Brownouts switch powered passives off; number keys reactivate them. Fixed orbit reactivation and a lightning catch-up burst after extended downtime.
- **Mastery:** nine nodes in three branches, purchased in Tab. One starting point, another every two Power levels. Pickup reach, fractional XP, drop odds, hull, regeneration, slow resistance, damage, brief stuns and Q aftershocks. HUD unspent counter; no new forced prompt. Run-only; menu preview cannot spend.
- **Menus:** equipment is fitted slots / collection / one selected item. Removed the quoted instructional footer. Costs and mechanics move to bounded hover panels. Stats use aligned rows and source damage bars. Loadout descriptions move to hover; fixed the fourth row overlapping the old description block. Main menu uses the actual robot with quieter navigation.
- **Art:** one medium-detail, code-native steel/teal/brass icon family across abilities, passives, mastery and equipment. Old generated illustrations are preserved but no longer mixed into the interface. No outside art was copied and no service or subscription was added.

Research observations and links are in [ITERATION_10.md](ITERATION_10.md). The current visual specification is [ART_STYLE_SCHEMA.md](ART_STYLE_SCHEMA.md).

## Start testing

Relaunch **Play MobaBot.io.cmd**. Choose **Loadout → Default kit** to use the complete new preset. The old R-nuke save migrates to E nuke / R laser, but custom Q/W choices are deliberately preserved. Existing equipment, music and preferences are not reset.

Try Q at a distant target, E then left-click, R then steer with RMB, and D through pressure. Hold Tab to spend mastery. Passives and later abilities still unlock gradually during the run; R unlocks at 70 combat seconds. Repair and energy consumables remain on 5/6.

### Balance probes

Final probes use normal health and starter equipment, deterministic seeds 2407–2409, real collisions and no forced boss kills. They cannot bank permanent rewards. These are diagnostic policies, not evidence of human fun or a target win rate.

| Policy | Results across three seeds |
| --- | --- |
| Idle | All lost, 8.77–10.12s |
| Stationary casting | All lost, 11.58–13.55s |
| Moving, indiscriminate casting | All lost in Levels 2–3, 124.47–291.77s |
| Moving, passives only | All lost in Levels 1–2, 27.10–141.57s |
| Adaptive movement, upgrades, mastery, casts, consumables and escapes | All won, 291.40–298.43s; 8–17 hull damage taken |

The adaptive policy deliberately invests in survival and energy, spends consumables, uses free escapes and saves short laser channels for boss recovery. It does not show that passive or other builds are equally viable. The older moving/casting probe intentionally does not understand the new mastery/channel system.

A separate real-renderer unattended run reached Level 2 and lost at 144.22s with 386 kills and 6 damage taken, without runtime errors. Its recorded frame intervals were approximately 7.18ms median / 11.51ms p95 on this RTX 5070, with at most 40 simultaneous enemies. This was not a rendered three-level completion or a lower-end hardware benchmark; the three-level completions above are simulation tests. Its build-label string was still the previous value when that process launched; the source now records `slice-10-mobabot`.

QA captures/logs remain in ignored `output/playtest-v10/`. No player-save or music files were modified by these fixtures. Storage tests that operate on actual local records were not run.

## Remaining judgement calls and later work

1. **Laser feel:** is steering suitably deliberate, or too sluggish? Boss openings are shorter than the five-second maximum, so cancellation is intentionally useful.
2. **Fairness:** are the shorter tells and three-hull charges readable? Does D reliably feel like an escape? Starting still dies quickly in the idle probe; test the first minute with a human before increasing pressure again.
3. **Resource clarity:** do the dim passive icons and brief brownout notice make energy management understandable without adding permanent help text?
4. **Art and menus:** does this shared icon detail level land between the two previous styles? Can you discover the hover details and mastery spending easily?

No additional levels, equipment-slot expansion or larger tree are needed before these answers. Later release work remains Windows export packaging, actual listening/audio balance, music-rights confirmation and lower-end hardware testing. These are not blockers for the current local demo.
