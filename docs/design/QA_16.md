# 0.16 · Keyboard, Forge and Painted icons

9 September 2026. This pass implements the latest nine-point feedback and the clarified **1–4/QWERT flexible, D/F movement, no Y** rule. Earlier QA notes are historical where they conflict.

## What changed

1. **In-run keyboard.** Home is Play / Equipment / Settings / Quit. New discoveries open a partial keyboard with pulsing legal positions; choose an empty position or explicitly replace a skill. Any general active, summon, ultimate or powered toggle fits 1–4/QWERT. D/F only accept movement. Tab → Overview → Arrange skills swaps positions without resetting rank, rarity, charges, recharge or ongoing casts. Replacing a channel cancels the removed channel. Orbit/Ricochet dependencies cannot be orphaned. There are enough logical banks for nine general actives or nine toggles, not just the old three/four split.
2. **Protected commands.** Attack move, camera recenter, Build and Settings remain non-selectable instruction tiles in the layout. Only these four actions have settings rebinding, outside ability/fixed keys. S still stops commanded attacks/movement, never independent gun fire. System-key collisions and invalid saved layouts are rejected.
3. **Painted / Base.** 49 current skill/toggle icons and 40 equipment icons were generated individually using a shared medium-detail cel/gouache machinery brief. Base preserves the original code-drawn skin. Settings switches between them. All source images remain in `assets/painted`; prompts and SHA-256 provenance accompany them. The laser was redrawn from a misleading saw into a beam. An opacity correction fixed the rocket source. Runtime imports are capped at 256 px with mipmaps; missing art falls back to Base. No AI runtime service or animated-sprite generation is required.
4. **Five-tier forging.** Eight slots, five tiers each; three identical pieces become one next-tier piece. No sets, rerolls, stars or forge currency fee. The equipped copy counts and advances automatically when forged. Gear grants health/resistance, plus speed on boots. A tier-4 piece grants one effective learned-skill rank; this applies once across gear, not per piece. Shared passive weapon tracks gain a rank; fixed effects gain 20% primary potency. Effective ranks cap at 10. Tier 5 additionally grants one following machine-gun companion, also non-stacking. The companion does not consume major-summon capacity.
5. **Display and UI.** Fullscreen is the default, with a windowed option. Power-up content is 80% of its previous linear size. Repeated fixed-category loadout explanation is gone. Equipment shows fitted items, five tier choices, three stat comparisons and Equip / Forge. Hover retains details. Settings has six options plus four protected-control bindings.
6. **Rewards.** Chest choices enter with a short staggered scale/fade; round rewards use an opening, settling industrial crate with brief rays. Reduced effects suppress the secondary motion. Nothing waits for an animation before accepting a choice.
7. **Resource visibility.** Compact outlined health/energy bars sit above the robot, with partial transparency and no duplicated numbers. The main resource HUD remains together. Above-player bars do not cover its face.
8. **Small balance pass.** Added an 18-skill single-target damage/geometry probe and documented ceilings, energy budgets and 12-phase moving-target tests. Torch damage rises from 24 to 26 over two seconds, with unchanged cost/cooldown/reach. Mirror damage follows it. No enemy damage, speed or spawn-pressure nerfs. These probes measure controlled geometry, not human accuracy or win rates; see [BALANCE_16.md](BALANCE_16.md).
9. **Restrained color.** Existing world sectors use blue-slate, warm foundry and violet-slate floor families. Skill icons use functional accent colors; hostile tells retain their priority. No wholesale environment or character replacement.

## Save conversion and protection

`mobabot_forge.json` is a separate v3 profile. The old expedition/equipment files are never overwritten. Migration pools old equipment into tier-1 copies of the same slot, including duplicates invested in stars, and stores the complete original snapshot. Old set identities are deliberately not treated as new power tiers. Credits and ascension access persist. Converted checkpoints discard old set effects and recalculate the current equipped stats.

Continue returns to a cleared-round checkpoint, not the exact combat frame. Flexible layouts, pending rewards and shop stock survive JSON round-trips. Starting over confirms checkpoint replacement. Failed transactions roll back; corrupt saves remain preserved and block writes. Automated fixtures never award player loot. Banked credits are retained but currently have no new forge sink.

## Owner feedback and honest assessment

The most recent local run record supports the owner's recollection: Stage 1, round 3, 185 kills, Power 15; lost after about 161 seconds of combat. About 103 seconds were recorded on upgrade screens. Older timing does not count all chest/camp time, so this is not total menu time. Raw player records remain local and are not committed.

The new equipment silhouettes are coherent at small sizes; the laser now reads distinctly from Q and the gun. Some neighbors still resemble each other: Reactor drop/Core strike and Ghost drive/Veil dash. Painted icons are more detailed than native world actors. That contrast is a deliberate experiment, not a solved art pipeline. The reward reveal is a short first pass, not elaborate loot choreography. Numeric quality scores remain at the previous baseline until owner feedback; see [QUALITY_BAR.md](QUALITY_BAR.md).

No external dependency or unanswered design choice blocks this iteration. Still unverified: packaged Windows release, clean-machine startup, music rights for publication, listening quality, low-end hardware and longer manual save/reload sessions.

## Best next three actions

1. **Test the choice loop.** Discover one skill, put it on a number key, swap it with Q, and return to combat without instructions. Measure choice time; shorten interruptions if the keyboard adds too much friction.
2. **Test clarity under pressure.** Compare Painted/Base during the first boss. Name any confusing skill pair and check whether above-player bars stay readable while kiting. Refine specific silhouettes/effects, not the whole pack again.
3. **Tune progression with real runs.** Compare the three classes and early energy starvation. Then test forge pacing: 3:1 fusion alone costs 81 tier-1 copies per tier-5 item; rarer higher-tier drops shorten this, but the long-term grind is not calibrated. Avoid blanket enemy nerfs until the source of difficulty is clear.
