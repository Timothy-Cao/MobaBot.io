# v0.8 — movement, shake and visual QA

September 8, 2026. Ready for the next human playtest. Scope remains Stage 1 / Levels 1–3. No extra campaign content, paid dependencies or account setup.

## Reproduced issues fixed

| Finding | Fix | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| Automatic damage pulses repeatedly triggered shake; only actors/effects shifted, not their hit indicators | Automatic pulses no longer shake. Hurt feedback is a small player-only angular recoil; world coordinates, enemy positions and aim geometry stay stable | Pulse-spam, bounded recoil, reduced-effects and decay tests; full/reduced impact captures |
| Dash velocity produced excessive player tilt | Clamp movement lean to the ordinary movement range | Movement captures and endpoint tests |
| Tank hit flashes ignored reduced effects | Honor reduced effects for the tank body as well as other actors | Paired identical full/reduced impact fixtures |
| Hold-to-steer retained a stale target when interrupted | Release held steering on pause, focus loss, inspection, settings and upgrade menus | Controller interruption tests. Deliberate single-click paths still survive inspection; S clears them |
| Mobility aimed directly outside a blocked arena edge spent charges without moving | Reject zero-travel blink/dash/lunge before spending energy, charges or cooldown | Both mobility presets at opposite corners; preview/cast agreement |
| Ground skills rejected an exact self-centered target | Mortar and stationary summons may target the player's feet; directionless line attacks remain rejected | Isolated cast tests |
| Aim previews ignored energy and other cast requirements | Shared target-point/readiness helpers; invalid preview is coral | Every ability checked against a real cast at zero/full energy; low-energy beam capture |
| Ordinary charger warning was narrower than collision coverage | Capsule warning includes swept collision width and endpoint caps | Charger fixture and existing swept-collision tests |
| Arena clamping could rotate a boss charge's actual segment away from its stored direction | Committed direction follows the clamped endpoint; renderer derives capsule normal from that same segment | Arena-edge charge test/captures at both zoom extremes |
| Required-target labels collided with the boss HUD and each other | Bottom labels stack upward and to the side of their arrow | Coincident-target geometry test and capture |
| Player was partly clipped or hidden under HUD at arena edges | Demo camera includes zoom-independent screen clearance; outside floor is darkened and existing boundary is visible. Playable bounds unchanged | Four corners × three zoom values, cursor-coordinate tests, edge captures; minimap camera box clipped to its bounds |

Camera update callback is explicitly physics-aligned. [Godot's Camera2D documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.5/classes/class_camera2d.html) distinguishes physics and idle callbacks. [Godot's jitter guidance](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/tutorials/rendering/jitter_stutter.html) distinguishes update-rate jitter from irregular frame delivery. This project draws actors from a custom simulation, so a project-wide transform-interpolation checkbox is not a complete interpolation solution for those drawings. No blanket “all hardware stutter eliminated” claim is made.

## Verification evidence

- Final suite: original smoke test plus **1,288 checks**, zero failures: 165 salvage / 102 MOBA / 55 iteration-04 / 476 iteration-05 / 43 demo / 88 readability / 359 motion QA.
- Movement matrix covers 30/60/120/144 **simulation update rates**, short and long destinations, four directions, rank-0/5/10 mobility, exact stopping, arena bounds, camera coordinates, interruption state and 30 repeated menu cycles. These are not measurements of subjective smoothness on four physical monitors.
- Layout audit measured **3,169 visible labels**, including menus and both presets' rank previews; no text-width overflow found. Width checks do not prove every possible overlap absent, so rendered inspection was also performed.
- GPU captures inspected at 960×540: damage/full and reduced effects, dash lean, unavailable aim, clamped boss charge, overlapping target pointers, upgrade cards, rank tree, stats, loadout, bindings, recovery and ordinary charger. A requested 1024×768 window preserved the 16:9 content layout; the viewport capture is 1024×576 and excludes the window's letterbox area.
- Full rendered Relaxed/2407 run completed: **291.48s, 950 kills, 4,424 scrap, Power 32, one hull hit**. Local wall/screen timing was saved. RTX 5070 prefix sample: 19,940 intervals after warmup; median 5.009ms / p95 7.79ms; peak 46 enemies within that sampling window. Not a hardware guarantee or whole-run peak.
- That rendered run preceded the final arena-camera/charge-normal and marker-layout refinements. Those refinements were then separately regression-tested and visually captured. The final headless suite includes the demo simulations. Storage integration passed with saved preferences preserved.
- The movement fixture's long invulnerability is only for isolated collision-free movement tests. Full-run health and boss simulations were not made invincible to manufacture wins.
