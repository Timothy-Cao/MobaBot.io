# 0.18 · Vanguard integrated playtest

9 September 2026. The owner explicitly requested a larger rewrite before judging it, superseding the earlier per-phase human stop gates. This delivers the integrated **Vanguard + progression + Practice** slice, not every item in the roadmap.

## Implemented

### Post-playtest Practice / HUD pass

- Practice-only floating damage numbers, enabled by default and toggled in Session. Short same-target tick coalescing and a 96-label cap limit clutter; fractional damage is retained.
- Immortal target dummies show per-target total damage and first-to-latest-hit DPS. Three simulation seconds without a hit clears the burst; paused configuration consumes no time. The last DPS holds during that grace, and a single instantaneous hit shows a dash rather than fabricated DPS. All damage sources count, including the independent gun. Dummies remain fixed and grant no kills/loot.
- Manual measurement/reset clears scratch meters. No save format or persistent preferences changed.
- Restrained HUD: compact resource/timer/mastery islands replace the full-width top slab; lower action-dock backing, smaller minimap, retained icon sizes/horizontal key positions and corrected toggle-Tab tooltip. No gameplay tuning or new art pack.

### Integrated kit

- New expeditions: fixed Vanguard. Permanent autonomous gun has no slot and ignores S/D. Q/D/F start learned; the other tools begin locked. Old checkpoints continue their original shared-pool rules; no forced conversion or lost skills.
- Left-click hammer: 0.20s commitment, 90° arc, 125 base reach, 1.05s base cadence. The outer head (64+ base distance) deals 38 unscaled damage, push and 0.25s ordinary-enemy stun; handle deals 9. Right-click/A can approach and repeat it. Attack mastery scales damage/speed/range, with matching visual reach. This replaces ranged commanded basics for Vanguard only.
- Q retains the distinct straight exploding rocket, with 80ms anticipation. W reaches 650 units and has a delayed 100-radius strike, 40-radius double-damage center, 38/76 unscaled damage; rank 5 stores two charges, rank 10 stuns ordinary enemies briefly.
- E is a short body slam: 210 nominal reach, collision stops at the first enemy/terrain, 95-radius 32-base-damage impact and push. Base protection suppresses touch only. Rank 5 adds stun. Rank 10 grants full protection during the dash and one second after enemy impact; a miss does not award that extra second.
- R is a 170-base-radius reactor impact after anticipation + 0.75s descent, 135 unscaled damage. Rank 5 shields the player when inside; rank 10 repeats the impact after 0.4s and stuns ordinary enemies. Click confirmation remains the default.
- D is held speed, not a timed invulnerability charge. Starts at +65% speed / 10 energy per second; ranks improve speed and efficiency. Release ends it. Blocks manual attacks/casts/redeployments while the gun, orbit and existing constructs continue. No initial activation cost or cooldown. Start sound and optional afterimages.
- F is instantaneous. An unreleased Q/W/R within its 80ms anticipation uses the new origin, preserving the original world aim point. Released effects do not move retroactively. Invalid terrain endpoints resolve to the near/far side according to midpoint penetration, using body clearance and a bounded search; blocked arena exits fall back safely. Endpoint poofs and a separate sound replace travel effects.
- 1: permanent powered orbit blades, close/fast versus far/slow. Ranks add damage, blades and milestone reach. Reactivation after energy shutdown is explicit via 1.
- 2: one replaceable Bulwark, durable and destructible, weak gun/local pulse, attracts at most four ordinary enemies inside 280 range. Bosses and ranged specialists retain player targeting.
- 3: one Reserve totem banks repair while away, then spends it on hull → energy → a capped-rate overflow shock. Redeployment clears stored value. Untargetable; expires after 35s.
- 4: one 5s Overclock well. Inside: free active energy and double recharge. Cooldown starts after expiry; it does not accelerate its own recharge. Untargetable.
- Level/chest opportunities queue upgrade/learn alternation. The front of the queue determines eligible slots; empty categories fall back to the other category. Maxed kits convert unspendable opportunities to field credits. Click small + controls or Ctrl+key, without pausing/casting. No replacement, cross-kit fitting or binding swaps. XP pacing is unchanged. Utility/mastery remain separate.
- Stable module/core/mobility HUD groups, short state labels and hover details. Consumables remain 5/6. T is unused in Vanguard. The old menu robot positioning fix is preserved.
- Practice: collapsible left dock, rank presets and per-key rank overrides, god/free-energy/recharge/frozen-enemy controls, time scale, clear/reset, simple total-DPS measurement, and paused world placements in Point/Line/Ring/Cluster formations. Overlapping/blocked batches reject atomically. Shift repeats, right-click/Esc cancel. A separate Legacy laboratory starts a clean shared-pool scratch run.
- Practice uses five substantial capsule obstacles in a compact test area within the existing arena. Collision, routing, projectiles, laser cover and spawn previews respect thickness. This is a greybox, not a finished map. Campaign terrain remains the prior rail layout.
- Practice kills grant no pickups, credits, chests or equipment. Campaign equipment/settings/music are preserved. New reward queues are validated within existing checkpoint payloads; no external save service or new permanent economy.

## Design defaults needing your verdict

1. D trades casting for speed and energy, without invulnerability. Does that feel like a useful choice or an unnecessary restriction?
2. Hammer remains deliberately committed. Its exposed basic-attack bot did poorly; do not assume a numbers pass solved melee risk.
3. R returns to a reactor drop for this kit. The old laser remains available in Legacy laboratory, not removed from the project.
4. Deferred choices are FIFO, capped at 256. MG upgrades use its own + rather than assigning another combat key. Does the flow stay noticeable but unobtrusive?
5. Totems create positioning decisions; healing storage and energy windows need real boss testing, not only isolated DPS.

## Remaining work / honest limits

- Marshal, Racer, visible Robot AI, offline rewards, new equipment economies and the parked combo kit are **not implemented**. Their specs remain available for follow-up.
- No campaign map rewrite, authored wave-grammar pass, original boss bodies, new bitmap pack or top-down robot rig.
- No general navmesh solver. Thick cover is capsule geometry; arbitrary touching/concave obstacle collections still need a broader terrain pass.
- No equipment editor, named Practice presets, detailed per-source results panel or replay recording. Current metric is total damage divided by elapsed simulation time; it is not a rolling hit-rate estimator.
- Some reused icons have close silhouettes; module 4's converter icon is a reuse, not bespoke art. Native effects need a human weight/readability verdict and audio listening.
- Fullscreen/export, low-end hardware and clean-machine certification remain outside this pass. The long 22-round route remains an open pacing concern.

## Suggested test

Start a **new expedition** to get Vanguard; Continue preserves an old build. Or choose Practice, rank 5, place ten bumpers in a cluster and one ranged threat. Compare hammer/Q/W/E; hold D while moving, then release into F/Q. Try Reserve and Overclock well against the boss. Judge this as one integrated prototype; report the three things that feel best and the three that need pruning.
